Testing: `@switchyard/test` finds shortest and simple model paths, reports state and
transition coverage, and ships `toBeIn` (vitest/jest), `assert.machineIn` (QUnit) and
`useManualClock`. `@switchyard/harnessed` turns model paths into tests that drive the real UI
through a harnessed harness.
