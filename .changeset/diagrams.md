Diagrams: `@switchyard/graph` draws machines as Mermaid, DOT and ELK-laid-out SVG, plus an
interface view and the live actor topology; `@switchyard/graph/viewer` is a live
`<switchyard-diagram>` element, and `@switchyard/ember/debug` wraps it as `<SwitchyardDebug>`.
Core names compiled guards and actions, and lists running child machines on `actors`.
